Launch-Ready Compliance Pack

A complete, turnkey AML/KYC documentation and operational package tailored specifically for payment service provider (PSP) and acquiring banking approval. Includes customized AML/CTF policies, Customer Acceptance Policies (CAP), enterprise risk assessments, and step-by-step Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).

Includes tiered player onboarding matrices (automated vs. manual triggers, Source of Funds/Wealth verification protocols, PEP and sanctions screening criteria), plus operational templates for SAR logging, suspicious activity escalation, and chargeback/fraud tracking.

Turnkey delivery: 2–3 weeks · Fixed-fee package · Built to pass PSP & banking due diligence

Compliance Tech Stack & API Integration

Hands-on evaluation, vendor selection, and technical configuration of your identity verification, transaction monitoring, and fraud prevention tooling (Sumsub, Veriff, SEON, ComplyAdvantage, or custom engines). Leveraging a systems engineering background, we bridge your platform's backend with compliance APIs.

We design and calibrate automated rules engines: deposit velocity limits, multi-accounting detection, proxy/VPN flags, payment anomaly triggers, and real-time transaction monitoring workflows to minimize friction for legitimate players while stopping fraudsters.

Implementation sprint · Architecture design, API mapping & rule calibration

Fractional MLRO & Compliance Lead

Dedicated, ongoing compliance leadership embedded directly with your founding team and operations. We act as your primary compliance point person: handling daily escalations, high-risk manual KYC and Source of Funds reviews, SAR filings, and ongoing reporting to payment processors, banks, and licensing authorities.

A flexible, cost-effective alternative to hiring an expensive full-time Head of Compliance before you have the volume to justify it — scaling support up or down as your Monthly Active Players (MAP) grow.

Monthly retainer · Direct founder/COO collaboration · Operational leadership

Sweepstakes & Emerging Model Governance

Specialist compliance architecture for US-facing dual-currency sweepstakes platforms (Gold Coins / Sweeps Coins) and offshore/crypto iGaming startups (Curaçao, Anjouan, Isle of Man, Tobique). We design defensible operating models: Alternate Method of Entry (AMOE) compliance, strict geo-blocking controls, and prize redemption KYC workflows.

We build robust audit trails and transaction monitoring safeguards specifically engineered to withstand heightened scrutiny from payment processors, card schemes, and acquiring banks in fluid regulatory environments.

Project-based or retainer add-on · Custom operational blueprint
