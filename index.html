Key Payment Underwriting Requirements We Address

Legal Exemption Alignment

Ensuring statutory sweepstakes or gaming exemption parameters are clearly documented for underwriters.

Compliant Terms & Rules

Tailored Terms of Service, Official Sweepstakes Rules, and clear AMOE disclosures.

Promotional Mechanics Distinction

Documenting dual-currency sweepstakes mechanics with complete transparency for risk assessors.

Technical Risk Controls

Documented device fingerprinting, velocity controls, and multi-account prevention systems.

Dispute & Chargeback SOPs

Established chargeback mitigation thresholds, refund triggers, and dispute representment workflows.

Billing Descriptor Alignment

Clear billing descriptor conventions to prevent customer confusion and bank inquiries.

Merchant Risk Profiling

Structuring corporate operational history and risk profiles to support fair reserve terms.

Complete Corporate Dossier

Verified ultimate beneficial ownership (UBO) structures, KYC compliance, and banking substance documentation.

About the Advisory

Specialized Regulatory & Operational Risk Advisory

Anchor Compliance Advisory bridges the gap between legal regulatory requirements and operational platform execution. We work directly with gaming founders, operators, and platform developers to establish complete compliance infrastructure.

Our engagements focus on preparing complete technical specifications, risk documentation, and operational procedures that satisfy payment acquirers and regulatory standards across US and international jurisdictions.

Jurisdictions & Frameworks

  • US Sweepstakes Compliance: Dual-currency mechanics, AMOE procedures, state statutory rules
  • UK & Isle of Man: Remote Gambling & Software Provider regulatory standards
  • Malta (MGA) & EU: Cross-border B2C and B2B compliance frameworks
  • Offshore Regimes: Curaçao, Anjouan, and Latin America (Brazil / Mexico) operational alignment
