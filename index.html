Services

Four engagements, from lightest
touch to deepest involvement

Each offering is structured around your operator's specific regulatory exposure, licence conditions, and risk appetite. Engagements are listed from the lightest entry point to the most involved — most clients begin at the top.

Start here

Independent Gap Assessment

A structured, end-to-end review of your current AML, KYC, and responsible gambling controls — measured against the specific expectations of your licensing authority (UKGC, MGA, or equivalent) and relevant frameworks including LCCP social responsibility codes, FATF Recommendations, and applicable AMLD provisions.

The deliverable is a prioritised findings report: each gap is mapped to the relevant regulatory requirement, assessed for severity, and paired with a concrete remediation step. The report is designed to be actionable by your compliance team immediately, not shelved as a theoretical exercise.

Typical delivery: 2–4 weeks · Fixed-fee engagement · No ongoing commitment required

Ongoing Compliance Retainer

Dedicated, part-time compliance support embedded in your operations on a recurring basis. Scope typically includes: policy and procedure maintenance as regulations evolve, SAR drafting and suspicious activity oversight, staff training on AML/KYC obligations, monitoring of regulatory change (UKGC licence condition updates, AMLD transposition, FATF mutual evaluation outcomes), and periodic internal control testing.

Designed as a cost-effective alternative to a full-time compliance hire — particularly suited to mid-size operators who need consistent senior compliance input without the overhead of a permanent headcount.

Monthly or quarterly cadence · Scope defined to your licence conditions

RG Programme Review & Uplift

A focused evaluation of your responsible gambling framework — covering customer interaction models, affordability and financial vulnerability assessments, self-exclusion processes (including GAMSTOP integration), and safer gambling tooling (deposit limits, reality checks, session controls).

Recommendations are mapped directly to LCCP Social Responsibility Code provisions and your specific licence conditions, ensuring each finding is tied to a concrete regulatory expectation rather than generic best practice. Where applicable, the review also considers emerging requirements from the UKGC's published compliance and enforcement priorities.

Standalone project or combined with a broader gap assessment

Regulator Engagement Support

Hands-on preparation for regulatory reviews, licence applications, licence condition change requests, or enforcement responses. Includes: document drafting (compliance policies, procedures, board reports), evidence packaging and narrative structuring, and advisory support to ensure your submissions are thorough, precise, and delivered on time.

This is the deepest level of engagement — typically engaged by operators who have already completed a gap assessment or retainer and need structured support for a specific regulatory interaction, whether that is a routine section 116 review or a formal enforcement response.

Project-based · Scoped to the specific regulatory interaction
