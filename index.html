01 Start here

Payment Risk, Fraud Prevention & Acquirer Onboarding

Securing processing rails and protecting merchant reserves requires demonstrable operational control. We configure end-to-end anti-fraud and merchant-readiness frameworks tailored to passing Tier-1 acquiring bank underwriting.

Chargeback & Payment Fraud Mitigation

Rapid velocity thresholds, BIN routing checks, mismatched billing data alerts, and stolen-card testing prevention.

PSP Due Diligence Dossiers

Comprehensive AML/CFT documentation, Customer Acceptance Policies (CAP), and operational SAR logging templates built for acquirer risk teams.

Deposit-to-Withdrawal Parity

Closed-loop payout rules and source-of-funds trigger points to stop payment cycling and card laundering.

02

Game Integrity, Slot Mechanics & Bonus Abuse Assessment

Promotional loopholes and game engine exploits can drain up to 20% of an operator's marketing budget. We perform systematic risk assessments to protect your platform margins against coordinated syndicates.

Slot & Game Exploit Audits

Identifying game mechanic vulnerabilities (e.g., feature-buy abuse, delayed round completion, bot farming, RTP manipulation patterns).

Bonus & Promo Abuse Prevention

Strategy design to eliminate multi-accounting for no-deposit bonuses, cross-betting, and low-risk wagering cycle schemes.

Network & Syndicate Detection

Implementing behavioral telemetry, shared device fingerprint clustering, and IP/proxy risk scoring at registration before bonuses are credited.

03

Back Office (BO) Risk Controls & Workflow Architecture

An operator's back office is the frontline defense. We audit and structure internal administrative tools to eliminate operational blind spots, internal collusion, and manual handling delays.

Back Office Role & Permission Hierarchy

Granular RBAC (Role-Based Access Control) to separate cashier approvals, player data access, and manual balance adjustments.

Manual Review & Escalation Queues

Concrete SOPs for risk agents handling suspicious bet spikes, multi-account links, and manual redemption approvals.

Audit Trails & Activity Logs

Implementing immutable logging for all administrative balance edits, KYC overrides, and payout releases.

04

RegTech Pipeline Architecture & API Integration

Translating regulatory and risk logic into backend developer tickets. We design and fine-tune your identity verification, AML screening, and fraud prevention tech stack.

Vendor Calibration

Configuration of engines such as Sumsub, Veriff, SEON, or ComplyAdvantage.

Automated Rule Logic

Tuning velocity limits, proxy/VPN detection, emulator checks, and synthetic identity flags to stop fraudsters without crushing legitimate player conversion.

05

Sweepstakes & Dual-Currency Governance

Specialized operational frameworks for North American social/sweepstakes platforms (Gold Coins / Sweeps Coins) and offshore licensing models (Curaçao, Anjouan).

Sweepstakes Integrity

Rigorous Alternative Method of Entry (AMOE) processing, strict geofencing controls, and tiered prize-redemption KYC workflows.

On-Chain Risk Controls

Crypto deposit provenance checks, mixer detection, and wallet screening for crypto-first operators.
