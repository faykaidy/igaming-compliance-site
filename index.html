Independent Gap Assessment

A comprehensive evaluation of your existing AML/CFT and Responsible Gambling operational frameworks against current regulator expectations (UKGC, MGA, and European jurisdictions) to identify compliance risks before they attract regulatory scrutiny.

AML/CFT Policy & Procedure Audit

Detailed review of Customer Due Diligence (CDD), Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD), Source of Funds (SoF), and Suspicious Activity Reporting (SAR) workflows.

Customer Interaction & RG Thresholds

Assessing automated trigger points, affordability indicators, and velocity-based RG markers against prevailing regulator benchmarks.

Actionable Remediation Roadmap

Prioritized findings matrix providing concrete operational steps to close identified gaps and satisfy regulatory standards.

Escalation & High-Risk Case Advisory

Expert review and advisory on complex EDD escalations, PEP assessments, and high-net-worth player Source of Wealth (SoW) dossiers.

03

RG Programme Review & Uplift

Targeted review and enhancement of your social responsibility framework, player protection policies, and customer interaction protocols to meet heightened regulatory scrutiny around gambling harms.

Markers of Harm & Telemetry Tuning

Refining automated behavioral indicators (session length, chasing losses, deposit velocity surges) for timely, defensible early intervention.

Customer Interaction Protocol Design

Developing clear operational SOPs for compliance teams conducting welfare checks, setting mandatory cooling-off periods, and enforcing self-exclusions.

Affordability & Vulnerability Frameworks

Designing robust customer affordability thresholds, vulnerability indicators, and third-party data integration workflows.

Regulator Engagement Support

Strategic preparation, evidence dossier structuring, and advisory support for regulatory audits, compliance assessments, licensing applications, and formal regulatory inquiries.

Audit Preparation & Evidence Dossiers

Structuring comprehensive compliance documentation, risk registers, and operational sampling packages ahead of regulator inspections.

Audit Trails & Governance Controls

Documenting immutable audit trails for administrative KYC overrides, customer limit adjustments, and operational compliance approvals.

Remediation & Response Structuring

Formulating clear, professional responses and remediation action plans following regulatory assessments or audit findings.

RegTech Vendor Configuration & Screening Accuracy

Fine-tuning and calibrating your identity verification (IDV), PEP/sanctions screening, and transaction monitoring tools to maximize regulatory accuracy and reduce screening errors.

Vendor Engine Calibration

PEP & Sanctions Screening Accuracy
