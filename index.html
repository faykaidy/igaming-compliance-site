Independent Gap Assessment

A structured review of your current AML, KYC, and RG controls against regulatory expectations. You receive a clear, prioritised report of risks and remediation steps — ideal as a first engagement with no ongoing commitment.

Ongoing Compliance Retainer

Dedicated, part‑time compliance support embedded in your operations. Covers policy maintenance, SAR oversight, staff training, and regulatory change monitoring — a cost‑effective alternative to a full‑time compliance hire.

RG Program Review & Uplift

End‑to‑end evaluation of your responsible gambling framework — customer interaction models, self‑exclusion processes, and safer gambling tooling — with actionable recommendations aligned to LCCP and licence conditions.

Regulator Engagement Support

Preparation for regulatory reviews, licence applications, or enforcement responses. Includes document drafting, evidence packaging, and advisory support to ensure your submissions are thorough, precise, and on time.
