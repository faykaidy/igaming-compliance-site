Acquirer Readiness

Your platform is ready. Your bank said no. We fix that.

Over 70% of first-time sweepstakes merchant applications are declined — not because the business is illegal, but because the application signals operational immaturity.

The 8 Underwriting Killers We Solve

01
No legal opinion letter alignment

Operator cannot articulate their statutory sweepstakes exemption to underwriters.

02
Generic Terms of Service

No sweepstakes-specific language, no AMOE disclosure, or copy-pasted terms.

03
No purchase ≠ entry distinction

Underwriter can't distinguish dual currency model from unregulated gambling.

04
Missing fraud controls

No device fingerprinting, velocity limits, or multi-account prevention rules.

05
No chargeback management plan

Lack of dispute thresholds, auto-refund triggers, or representment SOP.

06
Billing descriptor confusion

Descriptor reads ambiguous terms — instant red flag for card issuing banks.

07
No processing history defense

Brand-new entity flagged as maximum risk tier with crippling reserve requirements.

08
Incomplete corporate documentation

Missing beneficial ownership structure, KYC on UBOs, or banking substance.
